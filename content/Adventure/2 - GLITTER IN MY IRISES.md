<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Risk wakes groggy on the floor, covered in fabric shreds from the deteriorating shag carpet beneath him. He pulls one strand that had found its way into his eyelash and wipes the bleary sleep from his vision.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
<i>"Good afternoon, Mariner. The time is 14:46. There is a 43% likelihood of meteor showers today. The station has a total of 5 active work orders. You have 83 unread messages. Would you like me to-?"</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Just read out my most recent voicemail," he interrupts his virtual assistant as he rolls onto his side. The screen of Risk's comm unit flickers to life in his hand and displays text for him to read along.</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
<i>"Very well. Reading most recent message...Received from Zaria Kincade at 12:16."</i>
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Do NOT come to the market today. I repeat for you especially, Risk. DO NOT COME TO THE MARKET TODAY. You two really stirred shit up after showing your mugs around yesterday. I already stuck my neck out for you guys just to get you in the district, so don't fuck this up for me. Just keep your fuckin heads down on your way to tip top. K?"</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>Pleasant as always, Wraith.</i><br>
A pillow is lobbed at Risk's head.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Get up, sleeping beauty, they open in 5 hours,<br>and I know how much you wanna look good for the interview."</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Oh, shut up, I don't take <strong> that</strong> long."</i>
</p>
<hr>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey; opacity: 50%">
 | Face Danger (7,6 v S6+2): Strong Hit
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
With clear directions and clean obfuscation, Risk slinks through the Market's fringes with ease, quickly dipping down a neon-soaked staircase.
<br><br>
Venture Drive would almost feel like home, if the concrete was replaced with metal. The bunker's crumbling walls expose ancient piping, wires old and new. Scuffed and faded paint covers every surface in false skylines and yellow 'brick' paths. The air here is both better and worse than above. Dry, musty, but mostly clean, though it still carries a slight acidic sting only felt in fresh wounds.
<br><br>
Risk's dress shoes echo along the corridors, as he stares down at the directions on his comm. A few corners later, he nearly slams headfirst into a bedraggled man's back, only avoided by a sub-conscious push that nudged the guy to lean forward. Risk holds his breath, takes two careful steps back, and looks over the man's shoulder to see what's going on. In front of him, a long line hugs the left wall and snakes around the next turn. Risk cuts ahead of everyone else without them so much as batting an eye.
<br><br>
The entrance to Tip Top is oddly nothing more than a plain wooden door. No sign, no curtains; only velvet rope barriers and a bouncer to indicate this <i>being</i> a place at all. Of further note, the bouncer is a short, broad-shouldered woman with a candy orange vest as the only pop of color in her otherwise monochromatic attire. It doesn't show on her face, but Risk can feel her confusion and annoyance as he enters her line of sight but cannot actually see him. She stops the next person in line and takes off her AR frames, examining the lenses for cracks or faults. Naturally finding nothing wrong, she shrugs to herself, puts them back on, and signals the line to keep moving.
<br><br>
Risk, tucked around the corner, steels himself with a few deep breaths, before stepping back out into the hall. With each body he passes, every motion that brings him closer to the door, his mind is bombarded by anger and lust from those laying in wait. The bouncer only raises an eyebrow until he stands opposite her outside the velvet ropes. A light on her frames blinks twice. Then, she nods her head toward the door, and unclips one end of a rope for Risk to step through. The people in line barely start jeering before the bouncer swiftly cuts through them.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"You want a new toy to play with, then <i>be nice</i>!"
</p>
<hr>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Tip Top's interior stands in stark contrast to the rest of Venture Drive; life breathed back into one of the many derelict 'Atomitats' that number the halls. Just past a curtain of string lights are pops of circular booths that have been lovingly refurbished to their original saturated rainbow of colors, contrasted by the sharp metallic silver tables they wrap around. These sitting areas are inlaid in the ground, deeper the closer they are to the lounge's center. A surprisingly elaborate stage with a small runway takes up the northern half of the room. The carpet underfoot is a retro arcade-style, freshly installed.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey; opacity: 50%">
Character Oracle<br>
 | Species: Human<br>
 | Gender: Woman<br>
 | First Look: Aged, Threatened<br>
 | Role: Agent<br>
 | Goal: Escape a captor<br>
 | Revealed Aspects: Successful, Envious, Generous
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"Well, hey there, handsome," an older human woman monotones from the front desk at Risk's right. She's eyeing him up and down as he turns to face her, and he can feel a tension building in her chest the longer she looks.<br>
"You must be the new girl they talked about bringing on," she says, holding a hand out across the desk, palm up.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
His lips tighten. The attendant continues to stare expectantly. Eventually, Risk relents. A pistol is drawn from an inner pocket of his cropped sequin jacket, unloaded, and placed in the woman's hand.<br>
In one quick motion, her fingers clasp around his wrist, and she yanks him close. Her gaze stares daggers into his, her words cold.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"If you start working here- if I so much as hear a <i>peep</i> of trouble from you- you're going to have a lot more to worry about than a missing gun. These girls go through enough hell every day as it is. They don't need <i>you</i> messing with their heads, too." The agent releases her grasp, taking Risk's firearm and sliding it into a padlocked drawer behind the desk.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey; opacity: 50%">
Character Oracle<br>
 | Species: Human<br>
 | Gender: Woman<br>
 | First Look: Accented, Flashy<br>
 | Role: Entertainer<br>
 | Goal: Resolve a dispute<br>
 | Revealed Aspects: Intelligent, Stingy, Charismatic<br>
 | Name: Bruna Barbosa
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"Hurry up. Lady Barbosa's waiting in the back," she reclines in her chair, cocking her head to the door behind her.
</p>
<hr>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey; opacity: 50%">
 | Secure an Advantage (8,5 v H6+3): Strong Hit<br>
 | - Take Control: Add +1 to your next move<br>
 | - Prepare to act: Take +2 momentum
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Risk sits uncomfortably straight on an otherwise comfy loveseat in a room he would decidedly not call an office. Much like the rest of the establishment, Lady Barbosa's private quarters has been restored to its full Raygun Gothic glory and furnished to mirror that of a family living room. A bright orange box television on mute struggles to pick up the signal for a channel that only plays ads, anyway. Two glasses of red wine have been set on each side of an antique wood coffee table. The Lady herself is strewn across the chaise opposite Risk, sizing him in a similar but different way than her front desk staff.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"Please, dear, <i>relax</i>. Help yourself," silver fingers curl around the base of her own drink, "I promise you I'm not in the business of espionage. I never hurt a pretty face that doesn't deserve it first." She smiles to herself before having a sip. She is being honest.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Tentatively, Risk leans forward to take his own in hand, raising it up in a small toast to the Lady. He still does not drink, though he does allow himself to lean back, if only to make himself appear easily influenced. He feels her satisfaction at that. He does everything he can not to visibly grimace in turn.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"So, Mary-"
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Please, call me Risk,"</i> his manicured nails clink against glass. He takes a small, calculated sip of wine. Barbosa's eyebrows raise in mild amusement.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"I think the name Mary would get you a lot more clients, but if you insist- <i>Risk-</i>" her eyes alight, clearly reviewing his personal details. "I must ask because my curiosity has been absolutely killing me since we received your message the other turn. What brings someone like you to <i>Kingston</i> of all places?"
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>"With all due respect, Ms. Barbosa, I could ask the same of you. You don't strike me as the kind of woman that would willingly make her life in a district like this."</i> The two share a half-hearted chuckle. Risk adopts a shy smile. <i>"To answer your question, though, that unfortunately is not my secret to spill, but <strong>I</strong> can promise you are safer not knowing."</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"Those are some very dangerous words, my dear," the Lady purses her lips, tilting her drink at him, "Trouble I don't know about is trouble I don't want. So, you either tell me, or you leave." Her tone is calm, pointed.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Risk projects an air of anxiety. His free hand clenches in his lap.<br>
<i>"...Let's just say an unwitting actor recently returned home after a long tour, and his two bastard sons needed to make themselves scarce."</i> Barbosa's expression softens ever so slightly, unsurprised but sated. They both take another sip.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"I expected you must be on the run, since you're <i>clearly</i> not in-theater," she gestures at him generally, "But that is certainly...interesting." She's quiet for a few moments, thinking as she sits up and adjusts her sheer robes.<br>
"Well, consider yourself in good company."
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
The Lady pulls a box of cigarettes out from a drawer in the coffee table, offering one to Risk with a teasing smirk. He mirrors her expression, waves her off playfully.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"The why is not your business," she pauses to hold the cigarette between her lips and lights it. "But, I have my own disputes with the Wake. Blood has allowed me asylum here, in exchange for my business." A deep inhale, slow exhale of smoke into the ceiling fan overhead. "Speaking of which-" 
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Risk downs the rest of his wine. Barbosa smiles.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New; color: grey">
"-why don't you tell me what brings you to my humble establishment? What sort of skills do you have to offer? I can certainly think of a few, but I'd like to hear how creative <i>you</i> are."
</p>

 |  - Burn Momentum (9) = Weak Hit<br>
 |  - Ask the Oracle = Undiscovered Material
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
The old wooden staircase lets out over-exaggerated groans under Wraith's steps down to the first floor. As she slips under the thin chains holding up the construction sign, Chance pulls some marinated steak(?) out from the fridge. Glimmer Station's 'Glam Rock Radio' blasts in spite of the heavy static of a poor signal. A metal toolbox is loudly dropped to the floor.
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Thanks for helping out with cooking tonight,"</i> she tries her best to think to Chance through the noise, <i>"I really needed to get some work done up there, but you're brother's been making that kind of impossible."</i>
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"We've only been here like two turns,"</i><br>
he chuckles in her head.<br>
<i>"I know us coming wasn't exactly planned well in advance, but-"</i>
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
She claps a hand on his shoulder, cocking her head, narrowing her eyes.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
Chance turns to look at her with a smirk<br>and raises his arms in surrender.<br>
<i>"All I'm saying is that I think you're just looking for reasons to procrastinate, Z."</i>
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
 Both of their faces falter. One head turns away from the other.
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
"Well, at least I'm getting there, and we didn't have to listen to Risk whine the whole goddamn time."
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Wraith spins on the heels of her work boots and walks away, practically throwing herself onto the couch. Chance washes and dries his hands. The nearly unintelligible music slowly turns down before he steps over, plopping down next to her. For a long moment, the only sound between them is the crackling drone of electronics.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"Wraith, I-"</i>
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
"<strong>Don't-</strong>" the word comes out harsh, anticipatory. She takes a deep breath. Her shoulders slack.<br>"We're not having this conversation right now."
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
<i>"...Okay,"</i> he takes a moment.<br>
<i>"If not now, then...when?</i><br>
he chooses his words carefully,<br>
thinking them soft and slow. 
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
Wraith sighs.<br>
"Find Chimera. Then we'll talk."
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
Chance averts his gaze.
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
She starts getting back up to start on dinner.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New">
He sets a hand on her shoulder, standing up in her stead.
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">
A look of guilt crosses her face. But, she stays.
</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
The two make little conversation the rest of the evening.
</p>
