
<hr>
<p><strong>Session Date:</strong> Aug. 15, 2026</p>
<hr>
<p></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
   Enveloped in the broad vacuum of space, a cloaked shuttle makes its gradual descent toward Earth. Inside, crammed shoulder to shoulder, stand  dozens of government officials, shotgunning morning coffees and checking their itineraries for the day. A handful of armored soldiers position themselves at each corner.
   <br><br>
   Two figures sit in plain sight, backs pressed against one wall. Those around them shift and turn, eyes glossing directly over the colorful pair, and yet they remain unseen. The space they occupy is unconsciously left open.
   <br><br>
   <i>"...Times like these, I'm really glad we can't smell,"</i> thinks one with a smug grin.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Shut the fuck up,"</i> the other thinks back,<br> a laughter to his tone.</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"You know I'm right, though?"</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"You and I have been completely silent for the past hour,<br> and that's the first thing you're going to say to me?"</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"Better than just going 'Man, I wish we didn't have to drop out' for the fiftieth time."</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"..."</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">
Risk turns away from his brother, trying not to linger too much on his perfect ass-level view of everyone around them. At first, he considers resting his head back on his knees but instead decides to stand back up. He knows it isn't healthy. But, he can't help but let himself have one last look back at the station while it still feels close enough to touch.<br>
Eventually, Chance joins him, too.
<br><br>
Glimmer floats in the middle-distance; a crystalline asteroid hollowed out and serving as the shell for a 'luxury space station'. Two antennae stretch out from opposite sides, their ends idly blinking white. Out of line of sight hides one open hole, the remains of a project that will likely never be finished.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"...Do you think Dad's gonna be alright?"</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">Risk's eyes clench shut, fingers digging into his palm.<br>
<i>"He has to be."</i></p>
<hr>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">A gloved hand slams a glass of whiskey onto the kitchen counter, waking him from his tired daze. The staircase to the second floor is blocked off with an 'In Construction' sign. A generator hums somewhere in the backyard of this decrepit old house.</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">"You wanna spend your first turn here daydrinking, be my fuckin' guest," Wraith folds her arms across her chest, "As long as you pay for the damn bottle, I don't care."</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">Chance can only shake his head disapprovingly, as Risk knocks the drink back without hesitation.</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"I'll make sure he does,"<br> the former projects to the two's tentative host.</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"Myeh myeh myeh myeh myeh, can't a man wallow in peace?", he sticks his tongue out at his brother.</i></p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New">"Anymore of that, and I'm selling you to Tip Top," she tries and fails to threaten with a straight face.</p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">As the pair look at her confused, she turns her back and starts doing the dishes again. She absentmindedly blows away a blonde lock that falls into her face. Her faded blue robe haphazardly thrown on over her pajamas begins to slip off one shoulder.</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Soooo, besides MY generous hospitality," a clear thought cuts through the ambiance of Wraith's mind, "what do you think of the Arcade so far?" an air of sarcasm on her words.</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"It looks even worse up close."</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4font-family: Courier New; font-family: Courier New"><i>"It looks even worse up close."</i></p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Wouldn't be your first day on dirt, if you didn't have some drunk guy in the shuttle try pissing on you. That's what happened to me, anyway," she glances over her shoulder, "Actually, though, did the ride here go okay?"</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"The people were-"</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"-fine."</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"But, there was a bit of a hold-up as we got into Bristle."</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Something about a breach-"</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New"><i>"And the old chemical spill you warned us about. We were stuck in the metro car for an extra, like, 30 minutes."<br>
Risk feels Wraith's mood fall, a slight tension in her chest.
</i></p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Yeah, the uh. The cleaning effort has come a long way, but...there's still a lot more to go." Though, added more quietly at the end, she thinks, "I wish I didn't have to live here, anymore."</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">She tries to shake that thought away, but the anger and frustration that came with it stick to her like glue.</p>
<hr>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">The rest of the day is relatively uneventful with only a few outbursts from Risk to keep things interesting. The couch is pulled out with just enough room for the twins to share without much fuss, and there's plenty of good and bad entertainment between the TV and slightly water-damaged books on the shelves. The atmosphere is cozy yet nervous; the anxiety of welcoming near-strangers into your own home, despite your history.
<br><br>
An infomercial about mining robots flickers against Risk's closed eyelids. The other two sit awake, celebrating their moment of peace with a late-night coffee. A dim light over the countertop casts slight shadows down Chance's pale blue features.
</p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"So, Zari-" he catches himself,<br> "Wraith...I hate to ask, but<br> it's been driving me up the wall since we reached out for your help."<br> He takes a moment to collect himself,<br> using the addition of cream and suger to his coffee as an excuse.<br>"What's that favor you were talking about?"</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">She sighs, and they both share a drink, both wince as it goes down too hot.</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"As much as he pisses me off, I was gonna wait until THAT dipshit sobers up in the morning before I talked about it, but-" she takes another sip, staring daggers at the lump on her couch. "I know how much you hate not knowing things, so I'll at least give you this much."</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">Setting her mug aside, Wraith carefully pads past Risk toward her bedroom. She returns a few moments later with a wrinkled business card and slides it across the counter.
<br><br>
Kindred Finance<br>
Brendan Brade<br>
73 Cathedral Ave, Pleasant Grove<br>
62-851-0214<br>
</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"I won't get into all of it right now, but..." She picks her mug back up, taking a long swig before she continues. "I have-" she recoils a bit, "used to have...a girlfriend. Another voidborne that dropped out like us, but she was coming from a Mars station of all places.</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">Chance nods along quietly, sips his coffee that still isn't sweet enough. The house creaks against a stiff, fall breeze. Wraith's posture straightens suddenly, and she clears her throat, nearly rousing the drunken mess in the corner.</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"All you need to know for now is that her call-sign is Chimera, and this guy," her finger jabs the business card, "had something to do with wherever she's gone."</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Based on the business name, I'm guessing she got herself tied up in something she was trying to dig herself out of-"</i></p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"-and it didn't go well, yeah. Nice detective-work," she snips back a little harder than she meant to. "...I found this card in another pair of her pants. And I think he's got ties to the Wake because it's been fuckin' impossible getting any dirt on him." Her arms fold over her chest, staring a little too long at Brendan's name.</i></p>
<p style="text-align: right; color: #1C6EA4; font-family: Courier New"><i>"So...we're going to help you find her."<br>His words are not a question.<br>Simply a matter of fact.</i></p>
<p style="font-family: Courier New">Wraith looks back up at him, fighting with her tears.</p>
<p style="color: green; font-family: Courier New"><i>"Please, Chance.<br>"She's my world."</i></p>
