- - -
*At one time an agricultural source for Arcadia, this **Deserted Sub-District** at the district's western perimeter has been ravaged by pollution from a toxic waste accident that still lurks in the ancient ruins beneath the abandoned factories. While the few people still living here have barely just recovered from the spill, the streets are fraught with gunfire from an ongoing turf war between the Bloody Ascendancy and the invading Awakened Syndicate.*
- **Air Quality:** Toxic
- **Population:** Scarce
- **Features:** Disparate structures, Rustic architecture
- **Faction(s):** Bloody Ascendancy, Awakened Syndicate
- - -


## Catogan-Westwood
- - -
*Also simply called Cat-West, this neighborhood was only Catogan until the rapid expansion of the Cat-Can Market into Westwood, the abandoned factory neighborhood and epicenter of the toxic waste spill. Though, through costly exploration and cleaning efforts, Westwood was able to be reclaimed, even if only for nefarious purposes.*
- **Authority**: Bloody Ascendancy (Tolerant)
- **Projects:** Cat-Can Black Market
- **Trouble:** Overdue Shipment


## Bristle
- - -
*As the neighborhood of Kingston furthest away from where the toxic waste spilled, residents of Bristle have been working hard for decades, and have recently succeeded, at bringing life back to their land. However, due to the lawlessness of this sub-district left for dead almost a century ago, this neighborhood has now become fighting grounds over who will control it, likely for the purposes of growing elicit drugs.*
*Despite this, the residents of Bristle try to make the most of things, currently working on setting up some sort of festival to celebrate the return of life to the neighborhood.*
- **Authority:** None/Lawless
- **Projects:** Agriculture, Festival
- **Trouble:** Clash of gangs, Battle for leadership


## Venture Drive
- - -
*Only recently entering consideration as a sub-district of Kingston, Venture Drive has been the nickname given to the old ruins that fester beneath Cat-West. This Cold War-era residential bunker has largely been certain death for scavengers gambling their lives on a quick find, but through similar efforts as done for Westwood, some areas of Venture Drive have become "habitable". At least enough not to instantly die from toxic chemicals.*
*Has also become a bit of a hideout/neutral zone for the gangs fighting over Bristle, and certain sections beneath Cat-Can have opened for business.*
- **Authority:** None/Lawless
- **Projects:** Archaeology, Expansion
- **Trouble:** Toxic waste or pollution; Caught in the crossfire
