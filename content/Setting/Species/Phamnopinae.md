<p style="font-family: Courier New">
<i>A reclusive Voidborne species that makes their home in asteroid belts, moons, and low to zero-G planets. Known to have anti-memetic camouflage, empathic communication, and variable degrees of shape-changing capabilities. This collection of traits, combined with an isolationist culture, makes correspondence with Phem inconsistent, so our understanding of them is incredibly limited beyond individual accounts.
<br><br>
Often referred to as 'Deep-Space Sirens' or 'Anglerfish' after several hostile encounters over the decades, particularly in the early days of space travel. While these ship 'attacks' have all but ceased in recent years, Phem living openly among other species still face constant stigma as a result of this history. Additionally, in the time since First Contact, Phamnopinae that choose to leave their homes often quickly find employment in espionage, due to the particular combination of their abilities. While beneficial for top-level corporations and governing bodies, this has only sewn further fear and paranoia around the species.</i>
